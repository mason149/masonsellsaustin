Five unrestricted acres on the edge of Austin

A rare slice of unrestricted Central Texas land — five flat, usable acres just outside the city limits in Buda, with no floodplain and limitless room to grow. The property sits directly along the Travis County roadway expansion corridor, which makes it as compelling for a future commercial venture as it is for a private homestead.

At its center is a 1,481-square-foot, 3-bedroom, 2-bath home that has been thoughtfully remodeled and would serve equally well as a residence or a business office. Two outbuildings add serious utility: a 35×40 shop and an oversized 23×33 garage, with covered space for up to eight vehicles and parking for a dozen.

Offered privately, ahead of its public MLS debut, while final finishes are completed — a chance to get out in front of the market on a property that simply doesn't come along often.
