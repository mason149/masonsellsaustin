Buying, selling & investing in Austin

Does solar add value to a home in Austin?
In almost every case, having bought and sold several solar homes, it doesn't add the full cost. Even modern systems may only get half the cost back, and I've even seen appraisers value solar at zero.
From my video Should You Put Solar on your Roof in Austin Texas? | Solar as an Investment in 2022 (Oct 2022)
If I put a $40,000 solar system on my roof, will I get it back when I sell?
Make sure your finances line up so that you may only receive about $20,000 in actual value back, unless you own the house long enough to even out with your electric bill savings.
From my video Should You Put Solar on your Roof in Austin Texas? | Solar as an Investment in 2022 (Oct 2022)
Is solar a selling point?
It can be a huge selling point to some buyers, who may even choose your house over the competition, but that's sellability, not necessarily value.
From my video Should You Put Solar on your Roof in Austin Texas? | Solar as an Investment in 2022 (Oct 2022)
Should I lease or finance my solar panels?
Be very careful. Buyers are wary of taking over payments because it opens a new trade line that counts toward debt-to-income and could disqualify them. Most buyers will want you to pay it off anyway.
From my video Should You Put Solar on your Roof in Austin Texas? | Solar as an Investment in 2022 (Oct 2022)
What does it cost to transfer a solar loan when selling?
There are costs for the buyer and seller, and typically the seller has the larger portion. I've seen those be $5,000 before.
From my video Should You Put Solar on your Roof in Austin Texas? | Solar as an Investment in 2022 (Oct 2022)
Do solar warranties transfer to the new owner?
Most solar companies do provide transferable warranties, but make sure. If the warranty doesn't carry over, a buyer is likely to question buying the house.
From my video Should You Put Solar on your Roof in Austin Texas? | Solar as an Investment in 2022 (Oct 2022)
Do I have to pay back the solar tax credit if I sell?
Be aware of how long you have to keep the solar before you may have to pay tax credits back. I've seen a seller who sold within two years have to pay back the full credit, over $10,000.

If you're considering moving in the next couple of years, it's probably not a good idea at all, especially with a tax credit, loan or lease, unless you're paying outright.

What is a 2-1 rate buydown?
It's a 30-year fixed-rate mortgage where discount points are paid to buy down the rate for the first two years, typically about 2% lower the first year, 1% lower the second year, and then your fixed rate from year three on.

Is a 2-1 buydown an adjustable-rate mortgage?
No. It is a 30-year fixed-rate mortgage, not an adjustable rate mortgage.

Can you give an example of a 2-1 buydown?
If the fixed rate is 7%, you'd pay 5% interest the first year, 6% the second, and 7% onward.

What are the benefits of a 2-1 buydown?

Georgetown

Is Georgetown TX a good place to live?
I think so. It's one of my favorite suburbs around Austin, and because it was its own standalone town for so long, it doesn't live like a suburb. It has a historic square, live music on weekends and trails into downtown.
From my Living in Georgetown, Texas guide
How far is Georgetown from Austin?
About 30 miles north. From near the square, downtown Austin was about 40 minutes without traffic and an hour or more at rush hour as of April 2024.
From my Living in Georgetown, Texas guide
What are the biggest complaints about living in Georgetown?
By far the biggest gripe has been traffic on University Avenue (SH-29) at rush hour. The Southwest Bypass connection to 29, which opened in 2023, gave people another route to I-35.
From my Living in Georgetown, Texas guide
What school districts serve Georgetown TX?
Most neighborhoods are Georgetown ISD. Santa Rita Ranch is split with Liberty Hill ISD and Parkside on the River is split with Leander ISD, so check the exact address.
From my Living in Georgetown, Texas guide
What is the property tax rate in Georgetown TX?
It depends on the neighborhood. On my tours from 2024 to 2026, rates ran from about 1.78% (Highcrest Meadow, April 2024) to about 2.6% (the Leander ISD side of Parkside on the River, September 2025).
From my Living in Georgetown, Texas guide
Which Georgetown neighborhoods have the lowest tax rates?
Of the ones I've toured, Highcrest Meadow (1.78%, April 2024), the Retreat at San Gabriel (1.8%, February 2024), and Riverstone and Broken Oak (about 1.8%, June 2025) were the lowest.
From my Living in Georgetown, Texas guide
Is there a Costco in Georgetown TX?
Yes, on the north edge of Georgetown. Before it opened, the nearest was about 20 minutes away in Pflugerville or Cedar Park.
From my Living in Georgetown, Texas guide
Where do people shop for groceries in Georgetown?
The big HEB at Wolf Ranch Shopping Center opened in 2023, alongside Target, Chick-fil-A and Starbucks. Newer HEBs have gone in at Bar W Marketplace and near Williams Drive and Ronald Reagan.
From my Living in Georgetown, Texas guide
What are the 55+ communities in Georgetown?
Sun City Texas, with four 18-hole golf courses and homes from the low $200,000s to over $1 million (December 2024), and Santa Rita Regency, the Toll Brothers-built 55+ section of Santa Rita Ranch.
From my Living in Georgetown, Texas guide
Is Santa Rita Ranch in Georgetown?
It's in the Liberty Hill / Georgetown area of Williamson County, outside city limits. Addresses read Liberty Hill, Georgetown or sometimes Andice.
From my Living in Georgetown, Texas guide
What is there to do in Georgetown TX?
The square has restaurants, coffee shops and live music on Friday and Saturday nights. There's a trail from Lake Georgetown into the square, the San Gabriel River trail from Wolf Ranch, and free Blue Hole Park.
From my Living in Georgetown, Texas guide

Leander

Where is Leander, Texas?
About 30 miles northwest of downtown Austin, in Williamson County, just north of Cedar Park.
From my Living in Leander, Texas guide
Does Leander have public transit to Austin?
Yes. Leander and Cedar Park are the only Austin-area suburbs on the CapMetro MetroRail line, which runs into downtown Austin.
From my Living in Leander, Texas guide
What school district serves Leander?
Most of Leander is in Leander ISD, which I'd call one of the highest-rated districts in Texas. Some Leander addresses, like Bar W Ranch, are zoned to Liberty Hill ISD, so check the specific address.
From my Living in Leander, Texas guide
How big is Leander and how fast is it growing?
Roughly 86,000 people as of 2024, growing about 10% year over year.
From my Living in Leander, Texas guide
Is Leander a good place to live?
It suits families and downtown commuters especially well, thanks to the schools and the rail line. It is distinctly suburban, with fewer local employers, so most residents commute.
From my Living in Leander, Texas guide
What is the property tax rate in Leander TX?
It depends on the neighborhood. On my 2024–2026 tours, many City of Leander communities with no MUD or PID were about 2%, Palmera Ridge was 2.4% in May 2026 and Bar W Ranch was 2.7% in May 2026.
From my Living in Leander, Texas guide
Where can I find acre lots in Leander?
Northwest Leander. Greatwood is the under-the-radar resale option, and Leander Estates, Woodland Estates and Hilltop Ranch are newer acre-homesite communities nearby.
From my Living in Leander, Texas guide
What is Northline in Leander?
A large Domain-style, open-air mixed-use development set to become Leander's new downtown. As of August 2026, the residential side is open and the commercial buildings have gone vertical.
From my Living in Leander, Texas guide
How long is the commute from Leander to the Domain?
In October 2025 I drove from the Domain to South Brook in north Leander in about 25 minutes on the 183 toll road, off-peak and against traffic. Rush hour will be longer.
From my Living in Leander, Texas guide
Why do Leander homes have rock yards?
The City of Leander requires xeriscaping across all builders to cut back on residential watering. It's a Leander rule, not a builder choice.
From my Living in Leander, Texas guide
What does Leander actually feel like?
Honest read: Leander feels suburban, with strip centers, national franchises and master-planned communities rather than dense walkable blocks. Old Leander is where the town character lives.
From my Living in Leander, Texas guide

Round Rock

Is Round Rock a good place to live?
For a lot of families, yes. It was one of the fastest-growing suburbs in the country for a few years, and people are drawn to the schools, the parks and a location that's easy to get around from.
From my Is Round Rock a Good Place to Live? My Honest Guide guide
What is the population of Round Rock TX?
Almost 120,000 people as of my May 2024 tour. Almost two-thirds of households had three or more people, so it's a very family-heavy city.

What's the difference between west and east Round Rock?
The west side (Brushy Creek area) has more mature trees, rolling hills and trails, and it's easier to commute from, so there's a price premium. The east side has more going on, with downtown, Old Settlers Park, Dell Diamond and the Kalahari.

As of my December 2023 tour, about 30 to 35 minutes without traffic and 45 to 50 minutes at rush hour. From the west side or the south end near SH 45, the Domain is about 12 to 14 minutes.

Most of Round Rock is Round Rock ISD. Some Round Rock addresses aren't, though. Siena is zoned to Hutto ISD, so always check the specific address.
From my Is Round Rock a Good Place to Live? My Honest Guide guide
What is the property tax rate in Round Rock?
It depends on the neighborhood. On my 2024-2025 tours I saw 1.7% in Saul's Ranch, 1.77% in Avery Centre and Monarch on Main, 1.9% in Paloma Lake, about 2% in Cielo and 2.3% in Siena.
From my Is Round Rock a Good Place to Live? My Honest Guide guide
Is Round Rock safe?
Round Rock is known as one of the safest suburbs in the Austin area. I'd let my daughter ride her bike around Old Settlers Park and not really worry.
From my Is Round Rock a Good Place to Live? My Honest Guide guide
What are the best parks in Round Rock?
Old Settlers Park is the big one, with trails, fields, a lake, a water park and Music in the Park. Play for All Park is a hidden gem for kids, and the Brushy Creek Trail runs from Cedar Park to near downtown Round Rock.

Yes, which is rare for an Austin suburb. Main Street restaurants, live music, coffee shops, the new public library and trails are all close together, and the Depot Townhomes and Monarch on Main were built around that walkability.

Can you find acreage in Round Rock?
Yes. Hidden Ridge is a small, recently built community with acreage home sites backing up to Brushy Creek, 3,000 to 5,000 square foot homes, in about the $1 million to $2 million range as of May 2024.
From my Is Round Rock a Good Place to Live? My Honest Guide guide
What are the downsides of living in Round Rock?
I-35 traffic is the biggest gripe, though the 620 extension and flyovers helped. The west side carries a price premium, and tax rates and school districts vary by neighborhood.

Pflugerville

Is Pflugerville a good place to live?
My family and I live here and have no plans on leaving. It's a nice balance of quieter suburban life without being far from Austin, and it has a good reputation for being safe.
From my Living in Pflugerville, Texas guide
How far is Pflugerville from Austin?
It's the very first suburb north of Austin, right off I-35. As of October 2025 we're about 15 minutes from the Domain, so you avoid the long downtown commutes of suburbs like Leander.
From my Living in Pflugerville, Texas guide
What's the difference between east and west Pflugerville?
SH130 splits the city in half. The west side is older and more established, with mature trees and easy toll-free commuting. The east side has more new construction, easy toll road access and higher property taxes.
From my Living in Pflugerville, Texas guide
What is the property tax rate in Pflugerville?
As of October 2025, roughly 2% on the west side compared to about 2.6% in some east-side neighborhoods. Kuempel Estates is under 2%.
From my Living in Pflugerville, Texas guide
Do I have to use toll roads in Pflugerville?
On the west side you can avoid most of them. On the east side you'll use them. At $2.50 each way, that's five bucks a day if you commute five days a week.
From my Living in Pflugerville, Texas guide
What school district is Pflugerville in?
Neighborhoods I've toured like Falcon Pointe, Carmel and Lisso are in Pflugerville ISD. Around Falcon Pointe, Hendrickson High, Kelly Lane Middle and Murchison Elementary were all rated 8/10 or higher on GreatSchools as of October 2025.
From my Living in Pflugerville, Texas guide
Are there charter schools in Pflugerville?
Yes. Harmony, IDEA and a new BASIS K-12 campus are along Wells Branch, and Valor is nearby, technically in Austin. You have to apply and it can be competitive.
From my Living in Pflugerville, Texas guide
What's new or coming to Pflugerville?
A new downtown on FM 685 with a five-story city hall and a 140,000 sq ft rec center with a natatorium, expected in 2026 as of October 2025. The Kelly Lane extension and a new H-E-B off 130 also just opened.
From my Living in Pflugerville, Texas guide
Why is Pflugerville called the Trail Capital of Texas?
The governor signed off on it. As of October 2025 there are 70+ miles of trails including the ETJ, and reportedly no resident is more than a mile from one.
From my Living in Pflugerville, Texas guide
Can you swim in Lake Pflugerville?
Yes. The city built a sand beach, you can use non-motorized watercraft like kayaks and paddleboards, and it's stocked for fishing.
From my Living in Pflugerville, Texas guide
Which Pflugerville neighborhood has the best amenities?
Blackhawk, Pflugerville's golf course community. It has multiple amenity centers, pools, playgrounds, pocket parks and trails, and they're still building new construction there.
From my Living in Pflugerville, Texas guide
What should I know about buying an older home in Pflugerville?
Homes 20 to 30 years old are not bad at all, but that's typically when the AC, roof and kitchen need replacing. You get a lot of charm for it.

Liberty Hill is an Austin suburb northwest of the city along Highway 183, in Williamson County.

Manor

Is Manor TX a good place to live?
For a lot of people, yes. It's about the closest suburb on the east side of Austin, you get a lot of house for the money, and Manor Crossing brought H-E-B and Home Depot to town. The trade-offs are 290 traffic at rush hour and tax rates around 2.6% to 2.8%.
From my Living in Manor, Texas guide
How far is Manor from downtown Austin?
About 12 miles east of downtown Austin on Highway 290.
From my Living in Manor, Texas guide
Does Manor have an H-E-B?
Yes. The H-E-B at Manor Crossing had opened by my November 2025 tour. Before that, Walmart was Manor's only grocery store for a long time.
From my Living in Manor, Texas guide
What is Manor Crossing?
It's the retail development at 290 and FM 973 with H-E-B, Home Depot, Chick-fil-A, Chili's and more coming. It's an absolute game changer for daily life in Manor.

What is traffic like in Manor TX?
Highway 290 is really the only main way in and out, and it's also a route to Houston, so rush hour can be rough. Old Highway 20 and FM 973 are being improved, but some projects are years away.

Austin

What are the best neighborhoods in Austin TX?
It depends on what you want. My clients who want walkability love Allandale, Brentwood/Crestview and 78704, and families who want amenities often look at Circle C, Easton Park and Goodnight Ranch.

What is a site condo in Austin?
A lot of South Austin new construction is detached homes that are condos by legal definition, on private roads that are fire lanes. You own your home and land, but street parking is limited.

Allandale, Brentwood/Crestview, Mueller, 78704 neighborhoods like Travis Heights and Zilker, and newer spots like Foxfield and Goodnight Ranch, where you can walk to parks, coffee or groceries.

February is usually the coldest month, and winter storms usually bring ice, not snow. March through May is the wettest stretch, and August highs are near 100.
