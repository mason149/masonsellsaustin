78704: A Guide to Six South Austin Neighborhoods

78704, or "704," is the zip code for Central South Austin, and it takes in neighborhoods like Travis Heights, Bouldin Creek, Zilker, Barton Hills, South Lamar and Galindo. To me it's the true embodiment of everything Austin has been and is becoming. Each neighborhood has its own character, price point and trade-offs, so here's how I'd describe them after driving all six.

Why 78704 is so desirable

704 has most of the best restaurants in Austin, the live music, the local culture, and everything from high-end shopping to a food truck slinging tacos out of a dirt lot. I started this tour at Jo's Coffee on South Congress, which is about as 704 as it gets.

The neighborhoods are close together but they don't feel the same. Some are hilly with downtown views, some are flatter with newer construction, and prices change a lot from one to the next.

Travis Heights

Travis Heights has the highest average price per square foot of any 78704 neighborhood, and for good reason. It's one of Austin's oldest neighborhoods. It's hilly, and some homes have downtown views. You can walk to South Congress for shopping, restaurants and Equinox, yet the streets are quiet because there are almost no major cut-through roads.

The homes are a mix:

  • Tudor-style homes
  • Early-1900s homes renovated with modern updates
  • ADUs and garage apartments
  • Large new construction, 3,000 to 5,000 square feet on corner lots

Bouldin Creek

Bouldin Creek sits west of South First Street, and it tends to be one of the really good value communities in 78704. Honestly, I can't fully explain why, but a lot of opportunities open up here.

It's less hilly than Travis Heights, so you get fewer views, but there are mature trees and a lot more newer construction instead of homes that have been redone over time. You can walk to Bouldin Creek Cafe (fully vegetarian) and Elizabeth Street Cafe.

Zilker and Barton Hills

Zilker

Zilker is by far my favorite neighborhood in 704. You're next to Zilker Park, one of the city's largest parks, with off-leash sections, the restored Zilker Zephyr train, botanical gardens, sports fields and the Town Lake trail. My wife and I used to run Town Lake when we were dating. You can walk to Juliet and Chuy's.

The homes are often custom and architect-designed, some with downtown views. Getting around is easy via 2222 toward Westlake, MoPac, or Lamar (and the Whole Foods there). The trade-offs: Zilker is one of the more expensive parts of 78704, and traffic can be heavier depending on your street.

Barton Hills

Barton Hills is an absolute hidden gem that I don't think enough people talk about. It's for people who want South Austin but like how Westlake feels: quieter, more tucked away, with similar topography and home styles. A lot of older homes have been torn down and rebuilt into larger, architect-designed residences.

South Lamar and Galindo: more value

South Lamar

South Lamar is home to the Broken Spoke Saloon, and it's a newer part of town with more condo options. As of July 2024, it's one of the places in 78704 where you can get in under $1 million. It's bikeable, with Loro, Easy Tiger, Central Market, Target and In-N-Out nearby.

Galindo

Galindo has a lower price point than its neighbors to the north. You can walk to Summer Moon Coffee off South First, plus restaurants, bars and live music, and Spokesman is close by (my wife's favorite coffee shop in all of Austin). There's a community center and a tennis club. Galindo is right off 290, and it's probably an easier commute than most of the other neighborhoods on this list.

A 78704 home up close

I ended this tour inside one of my own 78704 listings, as an example of South Austin style. It had a blue exterior, solid hardwood floors, custom woodwork on the stairs and a design by a South Austin-based designer. Homes like this are why I'd tell you to walk through a few in person.

Which 78704 neighborhood fits you

  • Walk to South Congress, quiet streets, character homes: Travis Heights, if the budget allows.
  • Newer construction and good value: Bouldin Creek.
  • Park life and custom homes: Zilker.
  • Westlake feel without leaving South Austin: Barton Hills.
  • Under $1 million or a condo: South Lamar.
  • Easier commute and a lower price point: Galindo.

My honest take
78704 isn't one neighborhood. Each part has its own feel and price point, so the right fit depends on how you want to live. Zilker is my favorite for the park and the custom homes, and Barton Hills is the one I think people overlook most. If value matters, look at Bouldin Creek first, then South Lamar and Galindo, where you can still find options under a million dollars. Travis Heights costs the most per square foot, but walking to South Congress on quiet streets is hard to beat.— Mason Bleasdell
