Edgebrooke in Pflugerville: Brohn Homes With No MUD Tax

Edgebrooke is a Brohn Homes community at 2720 E Howard Lane, between SH 130 and I-35, right where Austin, Pflugerville and Manor meet. It has a Pflugerville address but sits just outside city limits, and that's a big part of why it works: as of 2026 the tax rate is 1.72%, nearly a full percent below most of the neighborhoods around it. I filmed my first look in July 2024, before a single home was started. When I went back in October 2026, it was built up and a lot of it had already sold.

Location: where Austin, Pflugerville and Manor meet

The location is the most compelling thing about Edgebrooke. You'll see a Pflugerville address, but you're right on the line where Austin, Pflugerville and Manor all meet, just barely outside city limits.

That gives you a nice balance. It lives quieter and more suburban than Austin, but you're not far from anything:

  • Samsung Austin Semiconductor is about 5 minutes away.
  • Downtown Austin is under 20 minutes on a Sunday morning. Rush hour will be longer.
  • The Domain is an easy commute from here too.

Compare that with neighborhoods farther into Pflugerville or Manor. An extra 10 or 15 minutes each way, twice a day, is an extra half hour you're sitting in traffic. That's a big reason I like this spot.

What's being built around it

Edgebrooke is right in the middle of some gigantic projects, and I think this area will be basically unrecognizable within the next five years. As of October 2026:

  • Old Gregg Brewing Company at Howard Post, founded by one of the people behind a newly Michelin-starred Austin restaurant. The food is incredible.
  • Lucky Lab Coffee is about to open right next door to it.
  • PopStroke just opened on The Pitch campus, minutes away.
  • East Village is exploding, with new restaurants opening all the time, including First Watch and a sushi spot, all within about 5 minutes.

From first look to built out

When I filmed my first look in July 2024, the streets and utilities were in and the lots were divided, but not one home had been started. About a year and a half later, it's grown up a lot. Not only did it build out incredibly quickly, a lot of these homes have already sold.

That tells you something about demand here: people who want to be closer in to Austin, but still want a more suburban life, are choosing this neighborhood.

Brohn Homes and the price

The whole community is built by Brohn Homes. As of October 2026, homes ran from the very high $200s up to about $400,000, depending on size, with plans from about 1,230 to 3,007 square feet in one and two stories.

At this price range, I think Brohn is bringing a nicer house out here than some of the other neighborhoods around it that I won't name. I've sold a lot of new construction, and I've worked with Brohn on multiple occasions, so I know how they work from contract to closing.

The 1.72% tax rate

Speaking of finances, this is the other big one. The tax rate in Edgebrooke is 1.72% as of 2026. Go a couple of minutes in either direction and most neighborhoods are almost a full percent higher.

The reason: Edgebrooke sits outside a lot of the MUD and PID taxes that apply to most of the neighborhoods around here. Being nearly a full percent lower than most of the competing neighborhoods makes a massive difference in affordability. Compared with a neighborhood like Evelyn, which runs close to 2.5%, it could save you a few hundred dollars a month.

Tax rates change every year, so verify the rate for the specific address you're looking at.

Schools and charter options

Edgebrooke is in Pflugerville ISD. As of the 2026 zoning, that's Dessau Elementary, Dessau Middle and Weiss High School.

Check this carefully. Go one neighborhood over and you're in Manor ISD, and sites like Zillow tend to pull the nearest schools without verifying zoning. Confirm with the district for any specific address.

The other interesting part: between Howard Lane and Wells Branch, you're close to a lot of publicly funded charter schools. IDEA, BASIS, Valor and Harmony are all within about 10 minutes. There are good early-childhood options nearby too, like Goddard. That's a lot of options that don't require private school.

Who Edgebrooke fits

Pros:

  • A 1.72% tax rate (2026), with no MUD or PID
  • About 5 minutes to Samsung and under 20 minutes to downtown off-peak
  • Brohn Homes quality from the high $200s to about $400K (October 2026)
  • Charter school options within about 10 minutes
  • Restaurants and entertainment growing fast all around it

Cons:

  • School zoning lines are tight here, with Manor ISD one neighborhood over, so you have to verify by address
  • It's outside city limits, so check what that means for the specific home you're buying
  • The whole area is under heavy construction and will be for a while

My honest take
Edgebrooke is one of the most compelling new communities in Northeast Austin. You're about 5 minutes from Samsung, under 20 from downtown on a good day, and the tax rate is nearly a full percent lower than most of what's around it. If you want to be close to things without paying the MUD tax that comes with most new construction out here, this is the one I'd look at first.
