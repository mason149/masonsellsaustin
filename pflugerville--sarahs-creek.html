Sarah's Creek in Pflugerville: Close-In New Construction With Gas

Sarah's Creek is an older Pflugerville neighborhood from around 2000 with a small new section built by Taylor Morrison. What makes it stand out is simple: new construction from a high-end builder, with gas utilities, about as close to Austin as you can get in this price range. It's about 5 minutes from my house, and I've sold client homes here.

By Mason Bleasdell, REALTOR® with Mark Martin and Company  ·  Updated September 2026

Why Sarah's Creek: location and gas

Location, location, location. This is about as close to central as you can get for a new-construction community with gas utilities, from a high-end builder, in this price range.

That gas point matters to some buyers. Other new communities nearby, like East Village around the corner, are all-electric. If gas is important to you, for efficiency or just preference, this is a great spot.

Commute and what's nearby

Getting around is easy:

  • Pecan Street takes you to I-35, and I-35 north to Round Rock.
  • Wells Branch gets you to MoPac.
  • SH130 is about a 7-minute drive, for the airport.
  • The Domain and downtown are easy drives.

Close by you've got West Pecan Coffee, Fogonero (a Venezuelan restaurant), an H-E-B 5 minutes away and Stone Hill, with Costco and Target, within 10 minutes.

Pflugerville is a suburb, but it really does have a life of its own. My family and I chose to live not far from here.

The homes and lots

The Taylor Morrison section is a small strip inside the larger, older Sarah's Creek neighborhood.

  • Lots: most are about 40 feet wide. Less yard to maintain, which is great if you like a lock-and-leave lifestyle.
  • Floor plans: 3 and 4 bedrooms, and 4 bedrooms plus a loft in some. Most homes are roughly the same square footage.
  • Style: more modern architecture, with windows maximized.
  • Sprinklers: front and back yards.

The model I toured was the Cadence floor plan. You're getting the build quality a high-end builder commands.

The condo structure, explained

Technically, these are detached condos. That sounds odd, so here's what it means.

You own your yard and your house, inside and out, exactly like a single-family home. The quirks are that the roads are private and owned by the HOA (the street signs say "private"), and there are no sidewalks.

What does that mean practically? Almost nothing.

Prices, HOA and tax rate

As of my October 2024 tour:

  • Prices: mid $400,000s, with some good incentives, especially on inventory homes.
  • HOA: $100 a month. That's a little higher because it's a condo association.
  • Tax rate: a staggeringly low 1.6%.

That low tax rate hugely offsets the higher HOA compared with a more typical $50 or $60 HOA.

Who Sarah's Creek fits

Pros:

  • Close-in location with easy routes to I-35, MoPac and SH130
  • Gas utilities in new construction
  • 1.6% tax rate
  • High-end builder
  • Low-maintenance lots

Cons:

  • Small yards, about 40-foot lots
  • Private roads and no sidewalks
  • HOA is higher than a typical single-family HOA

My honest take
Sarah's Creek is about as close to central as you'll find for new construction with gas utilities in this price range. The condo label sounds strange, but practically it means almost nothing: you own your house and yard like any single-family home. And a 1.6% tax rate more than makes up for the higher HOA.
