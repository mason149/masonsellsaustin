Loyola: Rare New Construction in East Austin

Loyola is a small MileStone new-construction community in East Austin, right off the 183 toll road, with single-family homes in the mid $500s as of January 2024. New construction in East Austin is pretty rare, especially in a real neighborhood setting like this. It's about 15 minutes to both downtown and the airport.

Location and East Austin lifestyle

Loyola is in East Austin, right off toll road 183, but tucked back from the road noise. As of January 2024, it was about 15 minutes to the airport and about 15 minutes to downtown.

Groceries, restaurants, coffee and gyms are all within 10 minutes max. You're also close to East Austin's trails and parks, restaurants, live music, jazz clubs and speakeasies.

Not condos, and the tandem garages

Two questions come up a lot with Loyola.

Are these condos? No. They're single-family homes, and you own your lot. Yards are smaller, since it's an urban community. The streets are not fire lanes, so guests can park on the street. That's a real difference from a lot of newer Austin communities.

Are the garages one-car? They look like one-car garages, but all of them are built as two-car tandems. A couple of floor plans have garage doors on both sides, so you could use half as an office or home gym.

Builder and construction

MileStone has been building all over the Austin area for a very long time. A few details I liked:

  • Hardie board (fiber cement) board and batten, which is termite and weather resistant
  • Lots of windows
  • 30-year architectural shingles, which matter because hail is what replaces most people's roofs here
  • Front porches, cottage-style fronts and fun front doors

Having a builder that's willing to spend a little extra money on things like that really does make a difference.

Prices, taxes and HOA

As of my January 2024 tour:

  • Prices: a price point in the mid $500s, which for East Austin is almost unbeatable
  • Tax rate: 1.8%. Being in an older part of Austin where the infrastructure is already there keeps it lower than suburbs like Manor.
  • HOA: only $60 a month

These numbers change, so confirm them before you buy.

Timing and availability

When I filmed, Loyola was brand new and small, with very few closed homes. The model home wasn't built yet. Lots were available, and inventory homes were expected around May-June 2024. Check with me for what's available now.

Who Loyola fits

Loyola fits people who want new construction close to downtown and the airport, in East Austin, without the fire-lane parking rules of a site condo. The trade-off is smaller yards.

My honest take
New construction in East Austin, to be honest, is a pretty rare thing, especially in a neighborhood setting like this. To have a development like this at a price point in the $500s is almost unbeatable. But there are houses for sale everywhere. What's most important is finding the right spot for you and your family.
