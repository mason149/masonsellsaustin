Carillon in Manor: Four Builders, RV Garages and a Split School District

Carillon is a new-construction master plan on the east side of Manor, off Highway 290, with four builders and homes from the mid $300,000s into the $600s. The key thing to know is that it sits right on the line between Manor ISD and Elgin ISD. I filmed my full tour in February 2025, and it's also in my 2025 Manor guide.

Amenities

Carillon's amenity center has:

  • A clubhouse with indoor and outdoor hosting space
  • A pool with shade covers. I love the shade, especially if you have small kids.
  • Pickleball courts
  • A covered playground
  • Pocket parks, sidewalks and walking paths

At the back there's preserve green space with a dry creek, and the trail system is now open.

Where Carillon is and how Manor is changing

Manor is about the closest suburb with an easy commute into Austin for the value. Carillon is on the east side, off 290.

Manor is changing very rapidly. When I filmed in February 2025, the nearest grocery was still Walmart, but the new development with H-E-B and Chick-fil-A had gone vertical. Give it a year and Manor won't live anything like it does now.

The real con is traffic. 290 can be rough at rush hour. Manor is working on more routes in and out, and the proposals are on the City of Manor website. Map your commute before you commit. It's farther than it looks on a map.

Builders and homes

Carillon has four builders, each with its own take on design, colors and materials:

  • D.R. Horton
  • Chesmar
  • Brightland
  • Richmond American

You'll find large single-story homes and easy three-car garages.

The standout is Richmond American's RV garage, designed to fit a Class A RV bus. Most people use it as a gym, an office or a place for car lifts. When I filmed in February 2025, it was one of very few places around Austin offering anything like it, and as far as I knew Richmond American was the only production builder building them in Texas.

Prices

One of the bigger selling points here is value. As of my February 2025 tour:

  • Brand-new homes in the mid $300,000s
  • Larger homes selling in the $500s and $600s, with heavily upgraded Chesmar homes at the top end

There's something for most budgets.

Schools: the Manor ISD and Elgin ISD split

This is the most important thing to understand about Carillon. The neighborhood sits on the line between Manor ISD and Elgin ISD. In February 2025, a little less than half went to Manor ISD and the rest to Elgin ISD.

Transferring districts generally isn't possible, so we need to know which house before you decide. Funny enough, the neighborhood I grew up in was the same: three houses down, you were in a different school district. Confirm zoning for the exact address.

Property taxes

Carillon is outside city limits, and the tax rate depends on which section you're in. As of February 2025 it ranged from 2.3% to 2.7%. 2.7% is on the upper end of anything around the Austin area, so factor it into your payment. Texas has no state income tax, which helps offset it.

My honest take
Carillon is about value. You get a real amenity center, four builders to choose from and an RV garage product you'll find in very few places around Austin, starting in the mid $300s. Common questions

What school district is Carillon in Manor?
It's split. As of February 2025, a little less than half of Carillon was Manor ISD and the rest Elgin ISD. Transfers generally aren't possible, so confirm by address.
What is the tax rate in Carillon?
Between 2.3% and 2.7% depending on the section, as of February 2025. 2.7% is on the high end for the Austin area.
Who builds in Carillon?
D.R. Horton, Chesmar, Brightland and Richmond American, as of February 2025.
How much are homes in Carillon?
As of February 2025, brand-new homes started in the mid $300,000s and larger homes sold in the $500s and $600s.
Does Carillon have RV garage homes?
Yes. Richmond American builds homes with an RV garage designed to fit a Class A RV bus. Many owners use it as a gym, office or for car lifts.
What amenities does Carillon have?
A clubhouse, a pool with shade covers, pickleball courts, a covered playground, pocket parks and a trail system through preserve green space.
What is the commute like from Carillon?
290 can be rough at rush hour. Map your commute, because it's farther than it looks on a map. Manor is working on more routes in and out.
Is Carillon inside Manor city limits?
No. Carillon is outside city limits, which is part of why tax rates vary by section.
