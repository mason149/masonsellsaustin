Lisso in Pflugerville: Taylor Morrison New Construction off Wells Branch

Lisso is a Taylor Morrison new-construction community off Wells Branch Parkway, just north of the Austin city line, with a Pflugerville address and Pflugerville ISD schools. I think it's one of the coolest locations around, and I'm putting my money where my mouth is: I live just down the street. As of February 2025, homes ran from the low $400s to just over $600,000.

Location and commute

Lisso sits just off Wells Branch Parkway, right north of the Austin border. You get a Pflugerville address and Pflugerville ISD, and you're literally 5 to 10 minutes from everything.

There's an Amazon warehouse you can see from the community. SH130 is easy to reach for the airport and the Tesla Gigafactory, and Stone Hill Shopping Center is down the road.

If you want a suburban feel but don't want to be all the way out in a true suburb, this is a really good balance.

Charter and private schools nearby

This is one of the best and most overlooked things about Lisso. Three public charter schools are within about a mile along Wells Branch:

  • IDEA
  • BASIS
  • Harmony

Austin Classical School and Brentwood Christian School are also very close. In my October 2025 Pflugerville guide, I noted the new BASIS campus at 10th Street and Wells Branch is K-12. Charter schools require an application and can be competitive, so plan ahead.

The neighborhood's zoned public schools are in Pflugerville ISD.

The builder: Taylor Morrison

Taylor Morrison is a very well-regarded premium builder across the country. You'll see stone work, stucco and premium products here. It's not your bottom-of-the-barrel, cheapest-everything builder.

At the design center you choose interior colors, cabinets, countertops and exterior elevations. When I filmed, there were also inventory homes with some really amazing deals.

Amenities and lots

As of February 2025, Lisso had:

  • A soccer field
  • A pickleball court (built)
  • A pool well underway, expected by that summer
  • A playground and a sandbox
  • Walking paths through greenbelts

Greenbelt lots were available, especially in the newly opened sections.

Homes, prices, HOA and taxes

Homes range from smaller single-stories up to about 3,000 sq ft. Some of the wider lots allow a 3-car garage.

As of my February 2025 tour:

  • Prices: low $400,000s to just over $600,000 for larger homes with upgrades like a 3-car garage.
  • HOA: $135 a quarter, so $40-something a month. Extremely reasonable for the amenities.
  • Tax rate: 2.1%. Some areas down the road, like ShadowGlen, were up near 2.4%. On a $600,000 house, that really adds up.

Who Lisso fits

Pros:

  • Close-in location just north of Austin
  • Three public charter schools within about a mile
  • Premium builder with design-center choices
  • Low HOA and a reasonable tax rate
  • Pool, pickleball, soccer field and greenbelt trails

Cons:

  • It's new, so expect construction nearby for a while
  • Charter schools require applications and aren't guaranteed
