Brentwood and Crestview: North Austin's Most Walkable Neighborhoods

Brentwood and Crestview are two adjoining neighborhoods just north of downtown Austin, known for walkability, mature trees and Austin ISD schools like McCallum High. As of December 2023, homes ran from around $600,000 for some 1970s originals to well above $2 million for new construction. They're the perfect bridge between suburban and urban living.

Location

Brentwood and Crestview run together directly north of downtown Austin. As of my December 2023 tour, downtown was about 10-15 minutes away, the airport about 20 minutes via the new 183 extension, and Mueller about 10 minutes.

The neighborhoods are tucked back from the freeways, with big mature trees and a neighborhood park, so they're quieter than a lot of urban areas.

Walkability

I cannot emphasize this enough. Walkability is probably the number one perk of this neighborhood.

Where Brentwood and Crestview meet, there's a little market area with:

  • Little Deli and a pizzeria
  • Violet Crown, a coffee shop and wine bar / social spot
  • A small grocery store, a barber shop and a beauty shop

You can also walk to Brentwood Social House and Stiles Switch BBQ, and H-E-B is around the corner, within walking or biking distance. There are bike lanes throughout, and the Crestview MetroRail station runs all the way downtown.

Schools

Brentwood and Crestview are in Austin ISD:

  • Brentwood Elementary, inside the neighborhood, which had just been fully renovated as of December 2023
  • Lamar Middle School
  • McCallum High School

Lamar and McCallum have some of the most sought-after fine arts programs in Austin ISD. Austin Classical School, a private school, is nearby, and there are several churches and child-care options in the area.

Taxes

The tax rate here is really low for Austin. It's an older part of the city with no major infrastructure projects going on, just maintenance. Ask for the exact rate on any home you're considering, since rates change every year.

Homes and prices

Most of the neighborhood was built in the 1970s, when Austin had about 300,000 people. A lot of those originals have been remodeled, and some are still owned by the original owners.

The new builds have a style that's become very Austin: board and batten and stucco. It's not quite modern farmhouse, and not super contemporary. It's a little warmer.

As of December 2023, prices ran from around $600,000 for some of the '70s houses to well above $2 million for new construction. At the time, I had a new-construction listing here: 3,300 sq ft, 5 bedrooms, full spray-foam insulation and hardwood floors throughout.

Who Brentwood and Crestview fit

People moving from East or West Coast cities are often surprised by how suburban and spread out Texas is. Brentwood and Crestview are the perfect bridge: a yard and a quiet street, with coffee, dinner, groceries and a train downtown within walking distance.

The trade-off is price. You're paying for the location, and many originals will need updates.

Common questions

Where are Brentwood and Crestview in Austin?
They run together directly north of downtown Austin, about 10-15 minutes from downtown and about 20 minutes from the airport.
Is Brentwood/Crestview walkable?
Yes, that's probably the number one perk. You can walk to Little Deli, Violet Crown, a pizzeria, a small grocery, Brentwood Social House, Stiles Switch BBQ and H-E-B.

Yes. The Crestview MetroRail station is right there and runs all the way downtown.
