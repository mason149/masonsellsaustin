Upper East End in Pflugerville: Contemporary Taylor Morrison Homes Near Parmer

Upper East End is a small Taylor Morrison community of contemporary-style homes one road over from Parmer, right around the corner from my house. It's tucked away and feels suburban, but you're very close to everything. When I filmed in June 2024 it was nearly built out, with homes in the very low $400s before incentives.

Where it is

Upper East End sits one road over from Parmer. From the top of the hill you can see the Samsung semiconductor plant across the road.

What's nearby:

  • East Village, a mixed-use development with retail, restaurants, shopping and apartment-style condos, was supposed to open in fall 2024. It's bicycle distance.
  • Northeast Metro Park, one of the biggest metropolitan parks in the Austin area, with a BMX spot, hiking and soccer games. BMX isn't my thing, but it's really cool.
  • Day-to-day needs within about 10 minutes, including a couple of H-E-Bs.

You're tucked away and it starts to feel suburban when you get here, but you're so close to everything.

The homes

Taylor Morrison is a national builder that's very well respected in Austin. Here they built in a more contemporary style, with modern finishes and exterior facades. You don't see that often in this area.

The homes are all between about 1,800 and 2,100 sq ft, laid out a few different ways with a few different options.

Greenbelt lots

Because of how the community is shaped, a lot of homes back to greenbelt. Some sit up high over a dry creek area, which gives you natural privacy in the backyard.

Tax rate, HOA and prices

As of my June 2024 tour:

  • Tax rate: 2%. East Village, across the road, has an extra MUD tax on top of that. If you're going to spend that money every month, you might as well put it toward your equity.
  • HOA: $119 a quarter. There are no amenities. The amenities are the location and what's nearby. The HOA maintains the trails and common areas.
  • Prices: the very low $400s before incentives, and some buyers negotiated down into the $300s.

Availability

When I filmed in June 2024, Upper East End was almost completely done with construction, so there weren't many new-build opportunities left. Going forward, expect most homes here to be resales. Reach out and I'll tell you what's available now.

Who Upper East End fits

Pros:

  • Close-in location near Parmer, Samsung and East Village
  • Contemporary style that's rare around here
  • 2% tax rate with no extra MUD tax
  • Low HOA
  • Greenbelt lots with natural privacy

Cons:

  • No pool or amenity center
  • Homes fall in a narrow size range
  • Few new builds left as of mid-2024

My honest take
I really like Upper East End. You get a contemporary Taylor Morrison home, a 2% tax rate with no MUD on top, and a low HOA, all a road away from Parmer. There's no pool, but the location is the amenity. All of that together at an affordable price is really rad for this area.
