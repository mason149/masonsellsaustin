About Mason

Who is Mason Bleasdell?

Mason Bleasdell is a REALTOR® with Mark Martin and Company (TREC #0716373) who helps people buy and sell homes in Georgetown, Leander, Round Rock, Pflugerville and the greater Austin area. He has more than $140M in career sales, including 148 homes in the last five years, and he's a 2026 Platinum Top 50 Finalist. He has also filmed hundreds of neighborhood and home tours on his YouTube channel.

You work with me. Not a team.

A lot of the busiest names in real estate are teams. You call the name on the sign, and then you're handed off to a buyer's agent, a showing agent or an inside sales rep you didn't pick.

That isn't how I work. When you hire me, you get me:

  • I'm the one showing you homes. I've already driven the neighborhoods and toured the model homes, and many of them are on my YouTube channel.
  • I'm the one negotiating your contract. Your deposit, your option period and your repairs are handled by the person who knows your deal best.
  • I'm the one who answers your call. Same number from the first question to the closing table: (512) 983-7331.

I have great people behind the scenes who keep the paperwork, photography and video moving. But your relationship, your advice and your negotiation come from me.

A marketer first, an agent always

Before real estate, I worked in automotive advertising, and that shows in how I sell homes. Every listing gets lifestyle-first video, professional photography and a real plan to put it in front of the right buyers, whatever the price point. You can see how I do it in my listing process video.

For buyers, I want you confident the whole way through: the right location, the right home at the right price, and a contract handled backward and forward so your deposit stays protected.

Where I work

I'm a native Austinite and I focus on the north and northeast suburbs, where I've spent the most time on the ground:

  • Georgetown, including Wolf Ranch, Parkside on the River and the Santa Rita Ranch area
  • Leander, including Bar W Ranch, Palmera Ridge and Leander Estates
  • Round Rock, including Paloma Lake, Avery Centre and Cat Hollow
  • Pflugerville, including Blackhawk, Falcon Pointe and Kuempel Estates

I also help clients across Austin, Cedar Park, Hutto, Manor, Liberty Hill and the Hill Country.
