What it's like to live in Pflugerville

My wife and I bought our first home here, and we have no plans on leaving. What sold us was the mix: location, value, the people, the parks and the food.

Pflugerville doesn't feel like a commuter town. It's tight-knit. You go into the grocery store and someone recognizes you and says hi. It's also a very diverse suburb.

The outdoors is a big part of life here. The governor signed off on Pflugerville as the official Trail Capital of Texas. As of October 2025 there are 70+ miles of trails, counting the ETJ, and reportedly no resident is more than a mile from one. Lake Pflugerville is a reservoir with a sand beach the city built. You can swim, kayak or paddleboard, and it's stocked for fishing. Northtown Park, with its championship disc golf course and dog parks, is one my family uses all the time.

The city is putting real money into itself. A new downtown on FM 685 is under construction, with a five-story city hall of more than 100,000 sq ft and a 140,000 sq ft rec center with a natatorium and a suspended indoor running track. As of October 2025 it's expected to open in 2026. There are also newer spots I like, including Crux Climbing Center, Spokesman Coffee, Spicy Boy and West Pecan Coffee.

The one downside? We don't have a Trader Joe's. I drive to the Arboretum.

How Pflugerville is laid out

I-35 runs along the west side, SH130 on the east and SH45 to the north. SH130 splits the city in half, and which side you pick shapes your life here.

West of SH130 is older and established. Small pockets like Windermere go back to the 1970s, and Mountain Creek is about 20 to 30 years old. You get mature trees, trails to the library and West Pecan Coffee, and it's easy to avoid toll roads. Homes that are 20 to 30 years old aren't bad at all. Just expect that the AC, the roof and often the kitchen will need replacing.

East of SH130 is where most of the new construction and master-planned communities are, including Blackhawk, Falcon Pointe and Avalon. You get easy toll road access. Stone Hill Shopping Center at SH130 and SH45 has Costco, Home Depot, Best Buy, Target, Torchy's, P. Terry's and In-N-Out. A new H-E-B opened off 130 on the east side, and the Kelly Lane extension east of 130 has completely transformed commute times.

Toll roads: on the east side you'll use them. If it's $2.50 each way, that's five bucks a day, five days a week. It adds up.

Schools: Neighborhoods I've toured like Falcon Pointe, Carmel and Lisso are in Pflugerville ISD. Around Falcon Pointe, Hendrickson High, Kelly Lane Middle and Murchison Elementary are all rated 8/10 or higher on GreatSchools as of October 2025, and Blackhawk has its own on-site elementary. There are also public charter schools nearby: Harmony on Wells Branch, Valor (technically in Austin), IDEA and a new BASIS K-12 campus at 10th St and Wells Branch. You have to apply, and it can be competitive.

Costs and property taxes

Property taxes are the number to watch here. As of October 2025, the rate is roughly 2% on the west side and about 2.6% in some east-side neighborhoods, where new infrastructure is still being paid for. That makes a pretty dramatic difference in affordability.

A few examples from my tours:

  • Kuempel Estates: 1.9% with no PID, as of August 2025. That's one of the lowest rates in Pflugerville.
  • Blackhawk: about 2.1% to 2.6% depending on the section, with the highest in new construction, as of February 2025.
  • Lisso: 2.1% as of February 2025.
  • Sarah's Creek (Taylor Morrison section) and Edgebrooke: 1.6% as of my 2024 tours.

A PID is basically a loan the developer takes out to put in roads and utilities up front, and it shows up in your tax rate.

On price, Pflugerville has long been a value compared to Austin. Back in December 2022, the average home for sale in Austin was $1,003,000 and in Pflugerville it was $536,000. As of 2025, I was touring homes from the low $300s up to new construction in the $800s and $900s.

Neighborhoods I've toured

  • Blackhawk is Pflugerville's original and largest master-planned golf course community, with an on-site elementary and new construction still going in.
  • The Grove at Blackhawk is Blackhawk's newest section, with larger lots and floor plans up to 4,300 sq ft.
  • Falcon Pointe has the Residents Club amenity center and Murchison Elementary, Kelly Lane Middle and Hendrickson High inside the neighborhood.
  • Avalon has a well-done amenity center, an on-site elementary school and quick access to Stone Hill.
  • Carmel is a nearly-new east-side community with its elementary, middle and high school all inside the neighborhood.
  • Kuempel Estates is a small new-construction pocket next to Carmel with a 1.9% tax rate and fiber internet included in the HOA.
  • Sorento sits across from Lake Pflugerville and has homes from standalone townhomes to about 3,800 sq ft.
  • Lisso is a Taylor Morrison community off Wells Branch Parkway, within about a mile of three public charter schools.
  • Sarah's Creek has a newer Taylor Morrison section with gas utilities, close to Austin.
  • Edgebrooke is a Brohn Homes community near The Pitch and East Village with a 1.6% tax rate.
  • Upper East End is a small Taylor Morrison community with contemporary homes, one road over from Parmer.
  • Enclave at Cele is technically in Cele, just east of Pflugerville, with large homes and 2.5- and 3-car garages.
  • Village at Northtown is a 76-home KB Home community off Wells Branch that backs to Northtown Park.

Who Pflugerville fits

Pflugerville fits you if you want a real suburb with a community feel, but don't want to be far from Austin. If you want mature trees and to skip the toll roads, look west of SH130. If you want new construction, big amenity centers and schools inside the neighborhood, look east, and budget for tolls and a higher tax rate.

Pflugerville questions I get asked

Is Pflugerville a good place to live?
My family and I live here and have no plans on leaving. It's a nice balance of quieter suburban life without being far from Austin, and it has a good reputation for being safe.
How far is Pflugerville from Austin?
It's the very first suburb north of Austin, right off I-35. As of October 2025 we're about 15 minutes from the Domain, so you avoid the long downtown commutes of suburbs like Leander.
What's the difference between east and west Pflugerville?
SH130 splits the city in half. The west side is older and more established, with mature trees and easy toll-free commuting. The east side has more new construction, easy toll road access and higher property taxes.
What is the property tax rate in Pflugerville?
As of October 2025, roughly 2% on the west side compared to about 2.6% in some east-side neighborhoods. Kuempel Estates is under 2%.
Do I have to use toll roads in Pflugerville?
On the west side you can avoid most of them. On the east side you'll use them. At $2.50 each way, that's five bucks a day if you commute five days a week.
What school district is Pflugerville in?
Neighborhoods I've toured like Falcon Pointe, Carmel and Lisso are in Pflugerville ISD. Around Falcon Pointe, Hendrickson High, Kelly Lane Middle and Murchison Elementary were all rated 8/10 or higher on GreatSchools as of October 2025.
Are there charter schools in Pflugerville?
Yes. Harmony, IDEA and a new BASIS K-12 campus are along Wells Branch, and Valor is nearby, technically in Austin. You have to apply and it can be competitive.
What's new or coming to Pflugerville?
A new downtown on FM 685 with a five-story city hall and a 140,000 sq ft rec center with a natatorium, expected in 2026 as of October 2025. The Kelly Lane extension and a new H-E-B off 130 also just opened.
Why is Pflugerville called the Trail Capital of Texas?
The governor signed off on it. As of October 2025 there are 70+ miles of trails including the ETJ, and reportedly no resident is more than a mile from one.
Can you swim in Lake Pflugerville?
Yes. The city built a sand beach, you can use non-motorized watercraft like kayaks and paddleboards, and it's stocked for fishing.
Which Pflugerville neighborhood has the best amenities?
Blackhawk, Pflugerville's golf course community. It has multiple amenity centers, pools, playgrounds, pocket parks and trails, and they're still building new construction there.
What should I know about buying an older home in Pflugerville?
Homes 20 to 30 years old are not bad at all, but that's typically when the AC, roof and kitchen need replacing. You get a lot of charm for it.
